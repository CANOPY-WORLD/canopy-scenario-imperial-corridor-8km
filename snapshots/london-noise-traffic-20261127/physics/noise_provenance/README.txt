Road-traffic noise map for the Imperial Corridor (8 km, 8 m cells, 3-5 m layer mean centred 4 m above ground).
63 Hz FVTD wave per 256 m tile (128 m halo) x UTD ray ratios for 125 Hz-8 kHz; CNOSSOS-EU emission from the
CANOPY SUMO run (600-1200 s, 1,000 synthetic vehicles): absolute levels are lower than real London traffic noise.
Published as ../noise.npy (scene.json layers.noise): frame 0 L_Aeq dB(A), frames 1-8 octave bands 63 Hz-8 kHz in dB.
Road geometry, per-road contributions and tile files are not published.
